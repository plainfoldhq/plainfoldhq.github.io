Federal Reserve bank holidays 2026

Federal Reserve Banks and Branches are closed on 10 weekdays in 2026. On these days Fedwire and FedACH payments are not processed, so many bank transfers move to the next business day.

Thursday, November 26, 2026Thanksgiving Day
Friday, December 25, 2026Christmas Day

The rule: a holiday on Sunday is observed on Monday; a holiday on Saturday is not moved, and Federal Reserve Banks are open the Friday before. That is the main difference from the federal employee calendar.

Independence Day (Saturday, July 4, 2026): federal offices close Friday, July 3, 2026, but Federal Reserve Banks are open that Friday.

Method: the 11 federal holidays with the Federal Reserve observance rule; checked against the holidays package's Federal Reserve calendar. Individual banks may close on other days. Confirm with the Federal Reserve's published holiday schedule.
