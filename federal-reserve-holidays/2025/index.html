Federal Reserve bank holidays 2025

Federal Reserve Banks and Branches are closed on 11 weekdays in 2025. On these days Fedwire and FedACH payments are not processed, so many bank transfers move to the next business day.

DateHoliday
Wednesday, January 1, 2025New Year's Day
Monday, January 20, 2025Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr.
Monday, February 17, 2025Washington's Birthday
Monday, May 26, 2025Memorial Day
Thursday, June 19, 2025Juneteenth National Independence Day
Friday, July 4, 2025Independence Day
Monday, September 1, 2025Labor Day
Monday, October 13, 2025Columbus Day
Tuesday, November 11, 2025Veterans Day
Thursday, November 27, 2025Thanksgiving Day
Thursday, December 25, 2025Christmas Day

The rule: a holiday on Sunday is observed on Monday; a holiday on Saturday is not moved, and Federal Reserve Banks are open the Friday before. That is the main difference from the federal employee calendar.

No federal holiday falls on a Saturday in 2025, so the Federal Reserve and federal employee calendars have the same weekday holidays this year.

Method: the 11 federal holidays with the Federal Reserve observance rule; checked against the holidays package's Federal Reserve calendar. Individual banks may close on other days. Confirm with the Federal Reserve's published holiday schedule.

In 2025, executive orders also closed federal executive offices on Thursday, January 9, 2025 (National Day of Mourning for former President Jimmy Carter); Wednesday, December 24, 2025 (Christmas Eve); Friday, December 26, 2025 (Day after Christmas). Those orders do not set the Federal Reserve Banks' schedule, so they are not in this table; check the Federal Reserve's own announcements for those dates.
