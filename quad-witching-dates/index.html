Quad witching dates 2025-2035

Quad witching (also called triple witching) is the third Friday of March, June, September and December, when monthly stock options expire along with quarterly stock index options and stock index futures. The dates below are the last trading day of standard monthly equity options: when the third Friday is a market holiday, it moves to the trading day before (marked *). Index futures and index options follow their own exchange rules (CME, Cboe); check them for those contracts.

Monthly expirations for each year: 2026, 2027, 2028; 2000-2050 in the options expiration hub.

Standard AM-settled index options (for example SPX, NDX and RUT monthlies) stop trading on the Thursday before the expiration Friday and settle on Friday morning; the dates here are for equity options. Computed from the current rules with NYSE holidays, not copied from exchange notices. Weekly, end-of-month and other non-standard expirations are not included. Future dates assume no new market holidays or rule changes; an unscheduled closure can move a date. Check Cboe or OCC before trading.
