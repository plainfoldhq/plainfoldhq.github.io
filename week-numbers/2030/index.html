December 31, 2030 is in week 1 of ISO year 2031.

Get the ISO week of a date in code:

Python      date(2030, 3, 15).isocalendar().week
PostgreSQL  extract(week from d), extract(isoyear from d)
SQL Server  DATEPART(iso_week, d)
MySQL       WEEK(d, 3), YEARWEEK(d, 3)
Excel       =ISOWEEKNUM(A1)

Use the ISO year, not the calendar year, next to the week number: dates near January 1 can belong to the previous or next ISO year. US-style week numbers (weeks starting Sunday, week 1 = the week of January 1, as in Excel's default WEEKNUM) can differ by one week.
