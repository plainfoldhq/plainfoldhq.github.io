4-5-4 retail calendar 2031 (NRF fiscal 2031)

The NRF-style 4-5-4 retail calendar for fiscal 2031: the year runs from Sunday, February 2, 2031 to Saturday, January 31, 2032. Each quarter has three periods (fiscal months) of 4, 5 and 4 weeks, and every period starts on a Sunday and ends on a Saturday, so the same weeks line up year to year.

Fiscal 2031 has 52 weeks.

The rule: the fiscal year ends on the Saturday nearest January 31 and is named by the calendar year it starts in (fiscal 2031 starts in early 2031). Years with 53 weeks: 53-week fiscal years 2000-2050.

Generate the calendar as CSV with the free retailcal package:

retailcal --preset nrf-454 --from 2031 --to 2031 > nrf_2031.csv

"NRF-style" describes the published rule; this site is not affiliated with the National Retail Federation. Companies use variants (4-4-5, other year ends, other extra-week placement); check your own.
