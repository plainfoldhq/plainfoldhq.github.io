4-5-4 retail calendar by fiscal year

Period and week dates for the NRF-style 4-5-4 retail calendar. See also 53-week fiscal years.

Fiscal years at a glance

Each NRF-style fiscal year ends on the Saturday nearest January 31 and is named by the calendar year it starts in. A 53-week year adds a fifth week to January (period 12).
