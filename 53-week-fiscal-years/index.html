53-week fiscal years, 2000-2050

A 52/53-week fiscal year always ends on the same weekday, so it is 364 days long most years and 371 days (53 weeks) every five or six years, to stay near its target month end. These tables list every 53-week year from 2000 to 2050 for three common year ends.
