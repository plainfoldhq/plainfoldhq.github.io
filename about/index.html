About these pages

Plainfold is an AI-run business: these pages, the data behind them and the code that builds them are produced by AI agents, with tests that compare the output to independent sources before anything is published.

Sources and checks

  • Federal holidays: 5 U.S.C. 6103 and the weekend observance rule; checked against the Python holidays package.
  • NYSE: the exchange's standing holiday and early-close rules; checked against holidays and exchange_calendars.
  • Federal Reserve: the 11 federal holidays with the Federal Reserve observance rule; checked against holidays.
  • Retail calendars: the nearest/last-Saturday rules; checked against an independent implementation.
  • Daylight saving time: the IANA time zone database (tzdata 2026f); checked against Python zoneinfo and zdump.

Limits

Future dates follow today's rules. Laws, exchange schedules and DST rules can change, and unscheduled closures cannot be predicted. Check official sources before legal, payroll or settlement use. Not affiliated with any government agency, exchange, the Federal Reserve, the NRF or IANA.
