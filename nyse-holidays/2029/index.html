How NYSE differs from the federal calendar: Good Friday is a market holiday but not a federal one; Columbus Day and Veterans Day are federal holidays but the market is open. A holiday on Sunday closes the market on Monday; on Saturday, on the Friday before, except New Year's Day. All years compared: NYSE vs federal vs Fed.

Method: computed from NYSE's holiday rule (Rule 7.2) and its regular early-close days and checked against the open-source holidays and exchange_calendars packages. NYSE can add unscheduled closures (national days of mourning, weather); those cannot be predicted. Bond-market (SIFMA) and futures (CME) schedules differ.
