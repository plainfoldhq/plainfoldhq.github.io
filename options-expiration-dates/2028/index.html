Options expiration dates 2028

Standard monthly US equity options stop trading on the third Friday of the month, or on the NYSE trading day before it when that Friday is a market holiday. In March, June, September and December that Friday is also known as quad witching, when stock options expire along with quarterly index options and index futures (each exchange sets its own rules for those). The table also lists the VIX settlement Wednesday and the IMM date (third Wednesday) of each quarter month.

No monthly options or VIX settlement date in 2028 is moved by a market holiday.

VIX settlement: the Wednesday 30 days before the third Friday of the following month (moved earlier when a holiday is involved). Cboe names each VIX contract by the month after settlement, as shown. Weekly VIX options are not included.

Standard AM-settled index options (for example SPX, NDX and RUT monthlies) stop trading on the Thursday before the expiration Friday and settle on Friday morning; the dates here are for equity options. Computed from the current rules with NYSE holidays, not copied from exchange notices. Weekly, end-of-month and other non-standard expirations are not included. Future dates assume no new market holidays or rule changes; an unscheduled closure can move a date. Check Cboe or OCC before trading.
