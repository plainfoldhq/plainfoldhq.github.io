Options expiration dates

Monthly US options expiration dates, quad witching days, VIX settlement dates and IMM dates by year: 2026, 2027, 2028. Quad witching days 2025-2035: one table.

Every expiration moved by a market holiday, 2000-2050

Good Friday and Juneteenth (a market holiday since 2022) are the usual reasons: when the third Friday is a holiday, options stop trading on Thursday. VIX settlement moves earlier when the Friday it is counted from, or the Wednesday itself, is a holiday. Future rows assume today's holiday rules.

Before February 2015, standard options formally expired on the Saturday after the third Friday; the dates here are the last trading day. VIX rows start in 2005; the 2005-2010 dates follow today's rule and were not checked against Cboe notices.

Standard AM-settled index options (for example SPX, NDX and RUT monthlies) stop trading on the Thursday before the expiration Friday and settle on Friday morning; the dates here are for equity options. Computed from the current rules with NYSE holidays, not copied from exchange notices. Weekly, end-of-month and other non-standard expirations are not included. Future dates assume no new market holidays or rule changes; an unscheduled closure can move a date. Check Cboe or OCC before trading.
