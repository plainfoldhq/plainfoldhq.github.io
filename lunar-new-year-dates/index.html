Lunar New Year dates 2000-2050

Lunar New Year 2027 (Chinese New Year, Spring Festival) is on Sat, Feb 6, 2027, the start of the Year of the Goat. It is the new moon that starts the first month of the Chinese calendar (usually the second new moon after the winter solstice), reckoned in China Standard Time (UTC+8), so it falls between January 21 and February 20.
