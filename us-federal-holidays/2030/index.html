US federal holidays 2030

The 11 US federal holiday days off in 2030, with the day federal employees actually get off. When a holiday falls on a Saturday it is observed the Friday before; on a Sunday, the Monday after (5 U.S.C. 6103(b); Executive Order 11582 for executive agencies).

Method: generated from the holiday list in 5 U.S.C. 6103 and the observance rule; checked against the open-source Python holidays package. Not included: one-off closures by executive order, which cannot be known in advance. State and local holidays differ.
