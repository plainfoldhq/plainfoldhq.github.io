US federal holidays 2025

The 11 US federal holiday days off in 2025, with the day federal employees actually get off. When a holiday falls on a Saturday it is observed the Friday before; on a Sunday, the Monday after (5 U.S.C. 6103(b); Executive Order 11582 for executive agencies).

No holiday falls on a weekend this year.

Banks follow the Federal Reserve schedule and the stock market follows NYSE rules, which differ: see Federal Reserve holidays 2025 and NYSE holidays 2025. Working days per month: business days in 2025.

Method: generated from the holiday list in 5 U.S.C. 6103 and the observance rule; checked against the open-source Python holidays package. Not included: Inauguration Day (January 20, 2025, which is also Martin Luther King Jr. Day), a holiday only for federal employees in the Washington, DC area. State and local holidays differ.

One-off closures in 2025: executive orders also closed most federal executive departments and agencies on Thursday, January 9, 2025 (National Day of Mourning for former President Jimmy Carter); Wednesday, December 24, 2025 (Christmas Eve); Friday, December 26, 2025 (Day after Christmas). These are not statutory holidays, so they are not in the table above.
