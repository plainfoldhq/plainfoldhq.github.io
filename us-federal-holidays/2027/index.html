US federal holidays 2027

The 12 US federal holiday days off in 2027, with the day federal employees actually get off. This includes New Year's Day 2028, which falls on a Saturday and is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027. When a holiday falls on a Saturday it is observed the Friday before; on a Sunday, the Monday after (5 U.S.C. 6103(b); Executive Order 11582 for executive agencies).
