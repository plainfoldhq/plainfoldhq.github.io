Holiday lists: federal, NYSE (early-close days count as trading days), Federal Reserve. Trading days per year, 2000-2050.

In Python, with the free usbizdays package:

from usbizdays import business_days_between
business_days_between("2030-01-01", "2031-01-01", calendar="nyse")  # 251

In pandas/numpy, with the free pandas-holiday-calendars package:

import numpy as np
from pandas_holiday_calendars import busdaycalendar
np.busday_count("2030-01-01", "2031-01-01", busdaycal=busdaycalendar("NYSECalendar"))  # 251

Counts are computed from the holiday lists on this site and checked against the holidays package. Future unscheduled closures are not included. Business days in a state or company calendar differ.
