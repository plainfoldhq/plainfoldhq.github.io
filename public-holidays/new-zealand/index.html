Days in lieu

When a holiday falls on a weekend, New Zealand gives a day off in lieu on these dates: April 27, 2026 (Anzac Day (observed)), December 28, 2026 (Boxing Day (observed)), January 4, 2027 (Day after New Year's Day (observed)), February 8, 2027 (Waitangi Day (observed)), April 26, 2027 (Anzac Day (observed)), December 27, 2027 (Christmas Day (observed)), December 28, 2027 (Boxing Day (observed)), January 3, 2028 (New Year's Day (observed)), January 4, 2028 (Day after New Year's Day (observed)), February 7, 2028 (Waitangi Day (observed)).

Method: dates and names from the open-source holidays package (public holidays only). For each year, the working-day holiday dates were compared with an independent implementation (date-holidays) and matched. Holidays on a weekend are listed but not part of that check. Dates marked estimated follow a lunar calendar and are confirmed by the government closer to the date. Future dates follow current law; governments can add or move holidays.
