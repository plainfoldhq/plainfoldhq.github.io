Days in lieu

When a holiday falls on a weekend, Japan gives a day off in lieu on these dates: May 6, 2026 (Substitute Holiday), September 22, 2026 (National Holiday), March 22, 2027 (Substitute Holiday).

Method: dates and names from the open-source holidays package (public holidays only). For each year, the working-day holiday dates were compared with an independent implementation (date-holidays and workalendar) and matched. Holidays on a weekend are listed but not part of that check. Dates marked estimated follow a lunar calendar and are confirmed by the government closer to the date. Future dates follow current law; governments can add or move holidays.
