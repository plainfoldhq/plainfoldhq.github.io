Days in lieu

No day in lieu applies in 2026-2028: holidays that fall on a weekend are not moved.

Method: dates and names from the open-source holidays package (public holidays only). For each year, the working-day holiday dates were compared with an independent implementation (workalendar) and matched. Holidays on a weekend are listed but not part of that check. Dates marked estimated follow a lunar calendar and are confirmed by the government closer to the date. Future dates follow current law; governments can add or move holidays.
