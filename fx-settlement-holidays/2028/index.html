Spot convention: T+2. For pairs with USD, a USD holiday on the first day does not count; the value date must be a business day in both currencies. For EURGBP, two business days of both currencies, then the first day that is also a USD business day. Some banks apply other rules: confirm with your counterparty. Holidays from the open-source holidays package, each currency-year checked against QuantLib. Future dates follow current rules; check official central-bank calendars before settlement use.
