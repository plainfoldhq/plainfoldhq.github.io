Bond market holidays 2026: SIFMA closures and early closes

The US bond market (Treasury, agency, corporate and municipal securities) follows the holiday schedule that SIFMA, the Securities Industry and Financial Markets Association, recommends. In 2026 the schedule has 11 full closures on weekdays and 6 early closes. It differs from the stock market: the bond market closes for Columbus Day and Veterans Day while the NYSE is open, and it is usually closed on Good Friday like the NYSE.

Notes for 2026

  • Good Friday (April 3): SIFMA recommended an early close at 12:00 p.m. ET instead of a full closure; the NYSE is closed that day. (SIFMA has made this exception in some earlier years when the monthly jobs report was released on Good Friday.)
  • Independence Day falls on a Saturday (July 4), so the bond market closes Friday, July 3.

Columbus Day (October 12) and Veterans Day are bond market holidays; the NYSE is open on both. Holidays that fall on a Sunday close the market on the Monday after. A Saturday holiday normally moves to the Friday before, except New Year's Day and Veterans Day.

These dates are SIFMA recommendations, not exchange rules, and SIFMA can revise them (for example when the monthly jobs report falls on Good Friday). Dates after 2027 are projected from SIFMA's standing rules until it publishes that year. Unscheduled closures (such as a national day of mourning) cannot be predicted. Dates come from pandas_market_calendars (SIFMAUS); closures are cross-checked with QuantLib (UnitedStates, GovernmentBond) and early closes with SIFMA's standing 2:00 p.m. rule. Check SIFMA's own published holiday schedule (sifma.org) before you settle trades or set cutoffs.
