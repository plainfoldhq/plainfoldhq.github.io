Years with 27 biweekly pay periods

A biweekly payroll usually has 26 paydays a year, but 52 two-week periods are 364 days, so for any given biweekly schedule a 27th payday falls in the same calendar year every 11 or 12 years. For salaried staff this matters: if pay per check is the annual salary divided by 26, staff receive 27/26 of the salary that year, and per-period benefit deductions can run one period too many. Counts here are by the date the pay is made, after moving weekend and bank-holiday paydays to the previous banking day; a holiday can add a payday (a January 1 payday paid on December 31) or remove one.

Each weekday has two alternating biweekly series: (a) contains the first such weekday of January 2026, (b) the second (for Fridays: January 2 and January 9, 2026). A weekly payroll has 52 paydays in most years and 53 in some, shown in the second table.

Pay dates by year: 2026, 2027, 2028.

Banking days: Monday to Friday except Federal Reserve Bank holidays (a Sunday holiday is observed Monday; a Saturday holiday is not moved, so the Friday before is a banking day). Dates on a weekend or bank holiday move to the previous banking day, a common payroll practice; some employers pay on the next one. The CSV also has a funding date 2 banking days before each payday, a common ACH lead time; your bank or payroll provider sets the real cutoff. State and company holidays are not applied.
