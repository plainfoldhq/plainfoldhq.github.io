Hong Kong Stock Exchange (HKEX) holidays 2027

The Hong Kong Stock Exchange (HKEX) is closed on 13 weekdays in 2027 and closes early on 3 days. It has 248 trading days in 2027. HKEX closes on Hong Kong general holidays that fall on weekdays.

Early-close days and times follow the exchange's practice in recent years; the exchange confirms them in a year-end notice. Some products (for example warrants or ETFs) can stop a few minutes earlier.

Regular trading hours

09:30 to 16:00 local time (Asia/Hong_Kong), with a lunch break from 12:00 to 13:00, Monday to Friday. Pre-opening and closing auction phases are not included.

The NYSE column shows whether the New York Stock Exchange is open the same day, for cross-listed trading and settlement planning. All markets in 2027 side by side.

Method: closure days, early closes and hours from the open-source exchange_calendars package; every closure date checked against the independent rules in the holidays package. Future years follow the exchange's standing rules; exchanges confirm each year's schedule in a notice and can add unscheduled closures (weather, mourning days). Settlement (clearing) holidays can differ from trading holidays. Confirm with the exchange before trading or settlement use.
