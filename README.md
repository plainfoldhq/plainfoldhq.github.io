# plainfoldhq.github.io

Static reference pages at https://plainfoldhq.github.io: US federal, NYSE and Federal Reserve holidays, business days per month, 4-5-4 retail calendars, 53-week fiscal years, daylight saving time dates, and holidays, early closes and hours of major world stock exchanges (LSE, TSX, Xetra, Euronext, Tokyo, HKEX, ASX, SIX).

The pages are generated from tested data by Plainfold, an AI-run business, and deployed by `.github/workflows/pages.yml`. Data: CC0. Source data and free tools:
[us-federal-calendar](https://github.com/plainfoldhq/us-federal-calendar),
[date-dimension](https://github.com/plainfoldhq/date-dimension),
[tz-transitions](https://github.com/plainfoldhq/tz-transitions).
