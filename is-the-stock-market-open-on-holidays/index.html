Is the stock market open on holidays? NYSE, federal offices and the Fed compared

US holidays do not close everything. Federal offices, the New York Stock Exchange and the Federal Reserve Banks each follow their own holiday rules, so on some days the stock market is open while banks and federal offices are closed, and the reverse. The tables list every weekday in 2025-2031 on which at least one of the three is closed.

The short answer

  • Columbus Day (Indigenous Peoples' Day) and Veterans Day: the stock market is open; federal offices and the Federal Reserve are closed (when Veterans Day falls on a Saturday, federal offices close the Friday before and the Fed stays open).
  • Good Friday: the stock market is closed; federal offices and the Federal Reserve are open.
  • A holiday on Sunday closes all three on the Monday after.
  • A holiday on Saturday: federal offices close the Friday before, the Federal Reserve stays open, and the NYSE closes the Friday before (if it is an NYSE holiday) except for New Year's Day.
  • All other federal holidays close all three.

"Closed (executive order)" means executive departments and agencies were closed by presidential order; essential staff still work.
