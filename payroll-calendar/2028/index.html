2028 payroll calendar

US payroll pay dates for 2028, moved for weekends and Federal Reserve bank holidays (the days ACH direct deposits do not settle). Banking days: Monday to Friday except Federal Reserve Bank holidays (a Sunday holiday is observed Monday; a Saturday holiday is not moved, so the Friday before is a banking day). Dates on a weekend or bank holiday move to the previous banking day, a common payroll practice; some employers pay on the next one. The CSV also has a funding date 2 banking days before each payday, a common ACH lead time; your bank or payroll provider sets the real cutoff. State and company holidays are not applied.
