Banking days: Monday to Friday except Federal Reserve Bank holidays (a Sunday holiday is observed Monday; a Saturday holiday is not moved, so the Friday before is a banking day). Dates on a weekend or bank holiday move to the previous banking day, the usual payroll practice; some employers pay on the next one. The funding date (2 banking days before) is a common ACH lead time; your bank or payroll provider sets the real cutoff. State and company holidays are not applied. Not legal, tax or payroll advice.
