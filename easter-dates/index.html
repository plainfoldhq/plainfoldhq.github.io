Easter dates 2000-2050

Easter 2027 is on Sun, Mar 28, 2027 (Western churches) and Sun, May 2, 2027 (Orthodox churches). Western Easter is the first Sunday after the ecclesiastical full moon on or after March 21 (Gregorian calendar), so it falls between March 22 and April 25. Orthodox churches apply the same rule to the Julian calendar, so their Easter is the same day or 1, 4 or 5 weeks later.

Moveable feasts that follow Western Easter, 2025-2035

These are fixed offsets from Easter Sunday. Good Friday, Easter Monday, Ascension Day and Whit Monday are public holidays in many European countries, Corpus Christi in some (parts of Germany, Austria, Poland, Portugal). Exchange closures differ: see the stock exchange holiday pages.
