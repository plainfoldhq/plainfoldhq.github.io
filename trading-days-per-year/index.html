NYSE trading days per year, 2000-2050

The number of days the New York Stock Exchange is open in each year from 2000 to 2050, next to the number of US federal and Federal Reserve business days. Most years have 251 or 252 NYSE trading days; the range here is 248 to 253. A year has 260 to 262 weekdays, minus the market holidays that fall on weekdays.

For annualizing daily returns, many analysts use 252 as a convention; the actual count varies by year as shown.

In Python, with the free usbizdays package:

from usbizdays import business_days_between
business_days_between("2026-01-01", "2027-01-01", calendar="nyse")

In pandas, with the free pandas-holiday-calendars package: pd.bdate_range(start, end, freq=business_day("NYSECalendar")) (NYSE, Federal Reserve and US federal calendars, 2000-2050).

Past years include unscheduled NYSE closures (September 11 attacks, Hurricane Sandy, national days of mourning). Future years count only scheduled holidays; unscheduled closures cannot be predicted. Early-close days count as trading days. Federal counts use statutory holidays only (executive-order closures are not subtracted). Checked against the open-source holidays and exchange_calendars packages.
