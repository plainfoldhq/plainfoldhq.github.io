Three-paycheck months 2026-2028

Paid every two weeks, you get 26 or 27 paychecks a year: two in most months and three in two of them (three months in a 27-paycheck year; a payday moved back for a bank holiday can also change which month it falls in). Find your row by your payday weekday and the January 2026 date in its series (count back or forward in steps of 14 days from any payday you know): each weekday has two alternating biweekly series.

Months are counted by the date the pay is made, after weekend and bank-holiday paydays move to the previous banking day (for example a January 1 payday paid on December 31 counts in December).

Every pay date: 2026, 2027, 2028. Years with 27 paychecks: 27 pay periods.

Banking days: Monday to Friday except Federal Reserve Bank holidays (a Sunday holiday is observed Monday; a Saturday holiday is not moved, so the Friday before is a banking day). Dates on a weekend or bank holiday move to the previous banking day, a common payroll practice; some employers pay on the next one. The CSV also has a funding date 2 banking days before each payday, a common ACH lead time; your bank or payroll provider sets the real cutoff. State and company holidays are not applied.
