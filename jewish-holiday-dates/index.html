Jewish holiday dates 2000-2050

In 2027: Passover begins Wed, Apr 21 at sundown, Rosh Hashanah Fri, Oct 1 at sundown, Yom Kippur Sun, Oct 10 at sundown.

The table gives the Gregorian date of the first full day of each holiday; every Jewish holiday begins at sundown the evening before. Passover 15 Nisan, Shavuot 6 Sivan, Rosh Hashanah 1 Tishrei, Yom Kippur 10 Tishrei, Sukkot 15 Tishrei, Hanukkah 25 Kislev (first of eight days). Outside Israel, Passover, Shavuot and Sukkot add extra days; Rosh Hashanah is two days everywhere.
